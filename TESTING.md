# AfterEarth Playtest

1. Open the repository in Replit or a local terminal.
2. Run `npm install`.
3. Run `npm run dev`.
4. Open the Vite URL.

## Current playable test loop
- Move with WASD.
- Hold Shift to sprint.
- Click the screen to capture mouse look.
- Press V for third-person camera.
- Press E to collect scrap/interact.
- Press I for backpack.
- Press M for the star map.
- Press L to launch directly to Verdantia for the first planet-transition test.
- Press R on Verdantia to collect plant fiber.
- Press F5 to save and F9 to load.

This is an early systems test build: the Earth facility and procedural planet environments are functional prototypes, while cinematic story, full cockpit controls, combat, and polished collision remain in development.
