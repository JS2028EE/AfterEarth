# AfterEarth — Complete Design Bible

## Core premise
Alex Mercer awakens in an abandoned Earth launch facility. A recovered spacecraft reveals three locked refuge worlds. The player must survive, repair the ship, investigate humanity's disappearance, and confront the Custodian AI.

## Main cast
- Alex Mercer — playable systems engineer.
- ECHO-7 — ship AI companion, practical but increasingly emotional.
- Dr. Mara Voss — archived scientist whose logs reveal the evacuation conspiracy.
- The Custodian — antagonist AI that controls gates, drones, weather systems, and ancient infrastructure.

## Progression
1. Escape the launch complex.
2. Restore power and recover the ship core.
3. Find launch coordinates.
4. Unlock Verdantia.
5. Recover cryogenic navigation technology.
6. Unlock Cryon.
7. Recover the Null Choir archive key.
8. Unlock Aridion.
9. Enter the orbital archive and decide whether to restore humanity, destroy the archive, or merge with the Custodian.

## Simulation systems
Movement, sprinting, fall damage, gravity, collision, terrain, swimming, temperature, oxygen, hunger, hydration, stamina, inventory weight, crafting, weapons, projectile damage, ship thrust, braking, boost, fuel, energy, shields, landing, takeoff, weather, storms, day/night, animal needs, predator/prey behavior, quests, checkpoints, save data, planet gates, and open-world postgame.

## Planet design
Each planet is divided into explorable regions rather than one empty map. Regions contain landmarks, resources, wildlife, environmental hazards, ruins, hidden logs, and story objectives.

## Art direction
Photorealistic sci-fi: physically based materials, volumetric fog, high-contrast lighting, wet surfaces, dense foliage, ice scattering, sandstorms, damaged industrial architecture, cockpit glass reflections, and restrained holographic UI.

## Controls
WASD movement, mouse look, Shift sprint, E interact, I inventory, M planetary map, V camera mode, Space jump/ship ascent, Ctrl descent, F boost, R repair/reload depending on context.
